The HolyArrs player bundles the following shader sources under their original licenses.

Anime4K v4.0.1 by bloc97, MIT
https://github.com/bloc97/Anime4K/releases/tag/v4.0.1

ArtCNN C4F16 and C4F16 DS v1.6.2 by Artoriuz, MIT
https://github.com/Artoriuz/ArtCNN/releases/tag/v1.6.2

FidelityFX Super Resolution 1.0 (EASU and RCAS) by AMD, MIT
https://github.com/GPUOpen-Effects/FidelityFX-FSR/blob/master/ffx-fsr/ffx_fsr1.h

SSimSuperRes by Shiandow, distributed by igv, LGPL-3.0-or-later
https://gist.github.com/igv/2364ffa6e81540f29cb7ab4c9bc05b6b

glsl-smaa 4.0.0 by Damien Seguin and the SMAA authors, MIT
https://github.com/dmnsgn/shaders-smaa
The three GLSL stages and lookup textures are in the adjacent smaa directory.

The application specializes these files for browser rendering in mpv-shaders.ts,
artcnn-shader.ts and smaa-shaders.ts. To replace a shader, change its source
file here, run app/frontend/scripts/generate-processing-shaders.mjs with the
Naga CLI and glslangValidator, then rebuild the application.
